About EasyTools India

Welcome to EasyTools India — a collection of online utilities designed to make everyday digital tasks simpler and more accessible.

Our aim: Everyday tools. Simple. Private. Free.

We want to make useful digital tasks easier to complete, especially on mobile devices.

What We Offer

Our collection includes tools for common tasks such as:

  • Image tools: Compress, resize, crop, convert, rotate, merge and add text watermarks to images.
  • PDF tools: Create PDFs from images, merge PDF files, split pages and rotate pages.
  • Calculators: Calculate age, percentages, discounts, estimated loan payments, GST and date differences.
  • Text tools: Count words, convert letter case, clean text and compare text passages.

Designed for Simplicity

We aim to keep our tools straightforward, with clear labels, useful controls and results that are easy to understand. The website is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Our Approach to Privacy

Our tools are designed to process supported files in your browser wherever technically possible. Some features may depend on external libraries or services, so we do not claim that every operation is entirely offline.

Please read our Privacy Policy to understand the website's privacy approach and the role of third-party services.

Free and Accessible Tools

We aim to make the available tools accessible without requiring an account for basic tasks. Individual features may have technical limitations, including supported file formats, file sizes and browser compatibility.

Continuous Improvement

EasyTools India is an evolving project. We aim to improve usability, reliability and clarity as the collection grows. If a tool does not work as expected, please let us know through our Contact page, if available.

Important Information

Our tools are general-purpose utilities. Always check the results before relying on them for important tasks. For more information, read our Disclaimer and Terms & Conditions.
